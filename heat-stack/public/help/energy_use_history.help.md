title: Help text for Energy Usage History

markdown: Data in this table were uploaded from a CSV file that the homeowner downloaded from their utility’s customer portal. Review the *whole-home UA* values for noticeable outliers, *i.e.* values that are noticeably higher or lower than most of the values. When you spot an outlier, try excluding it by checking the checkbox in the *Override Default* column and examine the *Standard Deviation of UA* value to see if it is significantly reduced by excluding that billing period. If it is not significantly reduced, uncheck the *Override Default* checkbox and repeat these steps for another outlier, if any.
