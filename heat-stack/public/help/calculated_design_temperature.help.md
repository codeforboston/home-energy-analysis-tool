title: Help for Calculated Design Temperature

markdown: The heating system demand at the design temperature is used to size a heating system. Standard practice is to use the 99% ASHRAE heating design temperature.  The calculated value approximates the 99% ASHRAE value, but may differ by a few degrees in some cases due to use of a different climate data source. 

The heating design temperature has a direct impact on the maximum and average heat load values reported below the Heating System Demand graph.
